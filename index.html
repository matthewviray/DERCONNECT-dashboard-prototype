DERConnect Dashboard · Energy & Anomaly Detection is an independent, interactive capstone prototype for campus building managers, informed by the Dashboard Team Milestone brief and the supplied energy and anomaly-detection papers.

This is an unofficial student prototype, not an operational DERConnect service.

All buildings, readings, anomalies, savings and model results are simulated. The 24-building, 236-meter workspace reflects the scale described in the brief. No live DERConnect feed or trained model is connected.

Greenhouse-gas estimates use an illustrative 0.23 kg CO₂e/kWh factor. Expected usage is a synthetic baseline; a flag is a prompt to investigate, not a confirmed fault.
